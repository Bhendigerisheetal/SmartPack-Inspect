1. Image & Label OCR
Digital Text Capture

Upload clear package labels. OpenCV enhances contrast and suppresses noise before Tesseract extracts declared text.

2. Field Extraction
Structured Parsing

Extracts standard declarations: Commodity Name, Net Quantity, MRP, Manufacturer/Packer, Date, and Consumer Care details.

3. Rule Evaluation
Decision-Support Assessment

Evaluates extracted fields against Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules and classifies findings with statutory reasons.

Compliance Status Criteria
COMPLIANT
All mandatory statutory declarations are present, readable, and align with applicable standards.
NEEDS REVIEW
Low OCR confidence, ambiguous text, or conditional field requires verification by an inspector.
POTENTIAL NON-COMPLIANCE
Mandatory declarations (e.g. Net Quantity or MRP) are absent or format violates packaging rules.
