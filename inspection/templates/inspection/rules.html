Legal Metrology Rules Repository

Statutory verification rules under Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
Dept. of Consumer Affairs, GoI
Rule Architecture & Statutory Sourcing

To ensure legal accuracy and prevent speculative checks, all inspection rules are mapped to specific sections of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011 (as amended).

Rule ID Target Declaration Statutory Requirement Applicability Official Source Reference
LM-R01 Name & Address of Manufacturer / Packer Name and complete postal address of the manufacturer, or packer, or manufacturer and packer. Required Rule 6(1)(a), LM (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
LM-R02 Common or Generic Commodity Name Generic name of the commodity contained in the package. Required Rule 6(1)(b), LM (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011
LM-R03 Net Quantity Net quantity in terms of standard unit of weight or measure (g, kg, ml, l) with prescribed font heights. Required Rule 6(1)(c) & Rule 12, LM Rules, 2011
LM-R04 Month & Year of Manufacture / Packing Month and year in which commodity is manufactured, packed or imported. Required Rule 6(1)(d), LM Rules, 2011
LM-R05 Maximum Retail Price (MRP) Retail sale price of the package inclusive of all taxes in format "MRP Rs. ... incl. of all taxes". Required Rule 6(1)(e), LM Rules, 2011
LM-R06 Consumer Care Details Name, address, telephone number and email address of person or office to be contacted for consumer complaints. Required Rule 6(1)(n), LM Rules, 2011
LM-R07 Country of Origin Mandatory declaration of country of origin if commodity is imported. Conditional Rule 6(1)(ab), 2017 Amendment
